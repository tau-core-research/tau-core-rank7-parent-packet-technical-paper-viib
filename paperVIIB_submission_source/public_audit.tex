\section{Public-source eligibility audit}

The accompanying reproducibility package freezes a finite source search as of
7 August 2026.  A candidate is eligible for a future independent
action--quantum endpoint score only if all six predeclared gates hold:
\begin{enumerate}[nosep]
\item an independently reconstructed electromagnetic or morphological leg;
\item quantum-state coherence, phase or tomography;
\item same-system joint indexing;
\item an independent positive endpoint action, energy or work measurement;
\item a complete standard comparator; and
\item a candidate-specific operational realization of the source-frozen,
gain-free Tau cross-readout law on independently measured observables.
\end{enumerate}
The script \path{scripts/audit_public_source_eligibility.py} contains all
35 candidate identifiers, public links, gate values and textual verdicts and
regenerates
\path{data/derived/public_source_eligibility_audit.json}.  Zero of 35
candidates pass all six gates.  The gate-pass counts in the listed order are
$(30,25,22,11,35,0)$.  After
Theorem~\ref{thm:p3supportportparent}, the abstract Tau law itself is frozen;
the last zero now records the absence of a candidate-specific independent
operational map from the common physical source into its typed observables and
whitening metric.  Even before that operational gate is applied, zero
candidates pass all five data/comparator gates; the nearest candidates pass
four.  Thus the endpoint-data packet and its common-source realization remain
independently incomplete.  This finite audit is a reproducible
classification of the located sources, not an exhaustive theorem about all
existing experiments; individual gate assignments remain open to correction
when stronger public metadata or raw records appear.  Failure of source
eligibility is an acquisition and identifiability limitation, not a null Tau
signal.  The frozen query families, inclusion rule, source-version policy and
the absence of external payload hashes are recorded in
\path{data/public_source_search_protocol.md}.

Consequently the direct rank-seven Nature test is frozen but not yet
executable: no primary residual is scored on an ineligible packet.  The
available Wen path-phase reconstruction is only an auxiliary branch control;
its morphology increment beyond scalar action is not significant under the
shared-kernel null ($\Delta R^2=0.1758$, $p=0.144$) and does not test or
falsify the complete P3 relation.

\paragraph{Minimal two-role falsifier.}
A first rejection does not require simultaneous reconstruction of all seven
roles.  For any predeclared distinct typed pair $(a,b)$, the proposed minimal
P3 law necessarily restricts to
\begin{equation}
 C_a^2=C_b^2=I,
 \qquad
 \{C_a,C_b\}=0
 \label{eq:minimalpairtest}
\end{equation}
after the same source-frozen whitening used by the full test.  Therefore a
statistically resolved nonzero anticommutator on one eligible pair rejects the
minimal rank-seven completion.  Eligibility still requires two independent
typed source directions and an informationally complete reconstruction of the
symmetrized operator product; a single-state expectation value is not enough.
A passing pair does not establish the remaining roles or rank seven.  The
reproducible focused audit in
\path{scripts/audit_minimal_pair_nature_test.py} finds zero eligible packets
among fourteen targeted public architectures.  This $0/14$ result narrows the
acquisition requirement and supplies no Tau pass or failure.

The already derived ROOT--TT same-preparation identity is not an independent
realization of this pair.  At frozen occupation and overlap it has the form
$T_{\rm TT}=N_OK\rho_{01}$, so its real two-component Jacobian is an
invertible rescaling of the ROOT-coherence Jacobian.  The stacked Jacobian
therefore has rank two, equal to either complex terminal separately, and adds
zero cross-terminal novelty rank.  This factorization is a physical
cross-terminal prediction, but counting it as two primitive source directions
would duplicate one source jet.  The executable test consequently requires
two independently controlled typed perturbations and process or sequential
data that reconstruct their symmetrized cross-response.

Nor can complete tomography confined to the quantum terminal replace the
missing typed leg.  If $L_1=A_1L_Q$ and $L_2=A_2L_Q$, the two maps may describe
independent quantum gates or coordinates, but both still descend through the
same $Q$ role.  Relabelling one as $M$ or $G$ after endpoint inspection would
violate source freeze.  The audited higher-order Quantum SWITCH process matrix
and donor-spin gate-set tomography packages are therefore near candidates,
not eligible cross-role packets: both reconstruct rich quantum operations but
publish no independently sourced metric, stress or morphology perturbation.

A closer hybrid candidate is a public source-data package in which a
superconducting qubit is read through a piezo-opto-mechanical transducer.  It contains transduction
characterization, prepared-qubit IQ records, optical-power scans, and
pump-dependent $T_1/T_2^*$ families on one device.  The release nevertheless
separates these into figure-level aggregate families: it provides neither an
independently reconstructed mechanical/stress state nor shared record keys
for an informationally complete quantum--mechanical symmetrized product.
Optical-pump dependence is therefore a controlled environmental response, not
yet a second primitive typed map.  The package is an acquisition template,
not an executable P3 pair test.

A mechanical carrier does not by itself evade the same-sector no-go.  If
$L_{\rm state}=A_sL_Q$ and $L_{\rm mech}=A_mL_Q$, both reconstructions descend
through one quantum role even when the carrier is a mechanical oscillator.  A
calibrated displacement control is likewise not an independent output map.
The audited ETH motional-grid-qubit release contains rich characteristic,
Wigner, Pauli and process tomography, but all records reconstruct one motional
quantum carrier~\cite{Fluehmann2019}.  The NIST amplified-motion release is closer because it joins
calibrated displacement, squeezing and spin response on common parameter rows;
nevertheless the displacement is a control and the oscillator output is
inferred through the spin/Q readout~\cite{Burd2019}.  Neither release supplies an independently
reconstructed mechanical or stress output whose differential adds rank after
conditioning on $L_Q$, nor the required symmetrized cross-product record.
The fourteenth and physically closest public candidate couples a membrane
oscillator to a distant atomic-spin oscillator and reconstructs a joint
Gaussian EPR witness~\cite{Thomas2021}.  Its released source files expose one
combined optical trajectory or spectrum.  The separately named mechanical,
atomic and backaction spectra are fitted model components rather than
independently measured output maps.  The package therefore does not certify
typed rank two or the informationally complete symmetrized product.

\paragraph{Federated mosaic identifiability no-go.}
Several incomplete experiments can constrain one common source Gram only
after independently certified source-frozen transports place their measured
objects in the same carrier representation and action unit.  Let
$G\in\mathcal G$ be the declared source-whitened Gram, let
$g=\operatorname{vech}G$, and write the constraints from packet $k$ as
$y_k=A_kg$.  For $A=\operatorname{stack}_kA_k$, the Gram is unique exactly
when
\begin{equation}
 \ker A\cap\operatorname{vech}(\mathcal G-\mathcal G)=\{0\}.
 \label{eq:federatedgramcriterion}
\end{equation}
The reproducible audit in
\path{scripts/audit_federated_gram_completion.py} applies this criterion to
the fourteen frozen pair candidates.  Even optimistically granting both
normalized $Q$ and $M$ marginals, the design for
$g=(G_{QQ},G_{MM},G_{QM})$ has rank $2/3$ and leaves $G_{QM}$ free.  All
positive-semidefinite matrices
\begin{equation}
 G(\rho)=\begin{pmatrix}1&\rho\\\rho&1\end{pmatrix},
 \qquad -1\leq\rho\leq1,
 \label{eq:federatedpairfamily}
\end{equation}
therefore reproduce those marginals.  In the seven-role control, the seven
diagonals plus a connected six-edge overlap tree have rank $13/28$ and leave
fifteen symmetric-Gram directions unobserved.  Connectivity alone is not
identifiability; fewer than complete pair coverage can suffice only under
additional algebraic or low-rank identities derived independently of the
endpoint data.

Scalar Gram closure is itself only necessary.  The anticommuting pair
$(Z,X)$ and the commuting pair $(Z\otimes I,I\otimes Z)$ have the same
identity normalized-trace Gram, while their anticommutators differ.  Thus no
synthetic Nature packet or Tau score may be formed by stacking unrelated
marginals.  A valid mosaic still needs common-carrier transport certificates,
at least one independently measured cross-role edge for the scalar pair, and
process or sequential tomography for the operator product.  The finite
verdict is
\texttt{FEDERATED\_MARGINALS\_DO\_NOT\_IDENTIFY\_CROSS\_ROLE\_GRAM}; it is an
identifiability no-go, not a null Tau result.

The reproducibility package is not limited to claim-string checks.  The script
\path{scripts/audit_theorem_certificates.py} independently computes finite
witnesses for the Schur response, the nonzero invariant-potential minimum, the
ROOT--TT Jacobian rank, overlapping terminal maps, the distinction between
activation and full occupation, and the normalized same-reduct counterpair.
These calculations certify representative finite instances; the analytic
proofs remain the source of the universal statements.  The separate script
\path{scripts/audit_rank7_parent_law.py} reconstructs the support
multiplicities, the successive stabilizer reductions, the independent-unit
and decoder rank controls, and the Clifford/equal-Gram counterpair.  It also
verifies a simultaneous zero of the normalized nonnegative action components,
which is the finite certificate for the variational-occupation step in
Theorem~\ref{thm:p3supportportparent}.
The federated audit independently verifies the pair and seven-role design
ranks, positive-semidefinite completion interval, resolution stability and
the equal-Gram commuting operator countermodel; all thirteen checks are
generated from the packaged pair ledger.

\paragraph{Code and data availability.}
The manuscript source, deterministic audit scripts, tests, derived JSON
ledgers and the frozen public-source search protocol are distributed in the
reproducibility archive accompanying this manuscript.  No observational
endpoint data are redistributed or fitted here; external candidate records
are identified by public links in the frozen audit ledger.  Running
\texttt{python3 scripts/reproduce.py} rebuilds the figures, theorem witnesses,
PDF, arXiv source archive and test suite from the packaged files.
