\begin{abstract}
This technical paper isolates the algebraic and source-law content previously
embedded in the joint gravity--matter--gauge manuscript. Starting from a
finite typed support with two ordered cotangent ports, one common unit and a
noncollapsing body decoder, we reconstruct a seven-dimensional physical
quotient. A pointed exterior-seed realization supplies the canonical cyclic
record carrier, while a faithful nontracial control proves that rank and
central weights alone do not fix its metric. An orthogonally invariant,
positive operator-square functional has the Clifford relation as its exact
zero set. We also prove a Tau-local subsystem-composition theorem with hidden
global-relation and crossing-rule controls, and construct a class-minimal
enriched law whose global minimum occupies the complete packet inside the
declared finite source-irredundant one-copy class. The unchanged narrow reduct
does not entail this law, and no theorem here proves unrestricted physical
base--seed realization, ambient-parent exhaustivity, Standard-Model
occupation, or a measured Tau-specific anomaly. A frozen public-source audit
finds no currently executable gain-free cross-terminal endpoint.
\end{abstract}

\section{Rank-seven occupation and finite-local support}

The rank-seven packet is physical only through a nonzero effective source,
not through algebraic availability alone. For any finite smooth local extra
irrep,
\[
 e_{*,\lambda}=
 (C_\lambda-B_\lambda^\dagger A^{-1}B_\lambda)^{-1}
 (k_\lambda-B_\lambda^\dagger A^{-1}j).
\]
Positive unloaded split irreps vanish, while stable loaded or cross-coupled
irreps are generated support. Inside the declared enriched class, the
simultaneous zero of the class-minimal law provides the required load and
occupation. This does not prove that the unrestricted physical base--seed
parent realizes that law or exclude global or rank-novel ambient extensions.

\section{Conclusion}

The missing object is no longer an unspecified ``complete rank-seven
incidence.'' The declared completion has an explicit support--port preimage,
a canonical cyclic record carrier, an operator-square selector and a
nonnegative class-minimal law whose minimum realizes and occupies the packet.
The accompanying controls show separately why rank, central weights, local
marginals, action additivity and the unchanged narrow reduct do not suffice.

This is a conditional internal closure, not unrestricted parent selection.
The decisive next result must therefore be either a deeper physical derivation
of the enriched law from the actual base--seed response or a source-frozen,
gain-free cross-terminal prediction that is not implied by standard physics.
No additional standard-limit recovery can substitute for either result.
