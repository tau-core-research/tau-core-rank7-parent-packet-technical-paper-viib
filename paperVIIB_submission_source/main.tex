\begin{abstract}
This technical paper isolates the algebraic and source-law content previously
embedded in the joint gravity--matter--gauge manuscript. Starting from a
finite typed support with two ordered cotangent ports, one common unit and a
noncollapsing body decoder, we reconstruct a seven-dimensional physical
quotient. A pointed exterior-seed realization supplies the canonical cyclic
record carrier, while a faithful nontracial control proves that rank and
central weights alone do not fix its metric. An orthogonally invariant,
positive operator-square functional has the Clifford relation as its exact
zero set. We also prove a Tau-local subsystem-composition theorem with hidden
global-relation and crossing-rule controls, and construct a class-minimal
enriched law whose global minimum occupies the complete packet inside the
declared finite source-irredundant one-copy class. The unchanged narrow reduct
does not entail this law, and no theorem here proves unrestricted physical
base--seed realization, ambient-parent exhaustivity, Standard-Model
occupation, or a measured Tau-specific anomaly. A frozen public-source audit
finds no currently executable gain-free cross-terminal endpoint.
\end{abstract}

\section{Rank-seven occupation and finite-local support}

The rank-seven packet is physical only through a nonzero effective source,
not through algebraic availability alone. For any finite smooth local extra
irrep,
\[
 e_{*,\lambda}=
 (C_\lambda-B_\lambda^\dagger A^{-1}B_\lambda)^{-1}
 (k_\lambda-B_\lambda^\dagger A^{-1}j).
\]
Positive unloaded split irreps vanish, while stable loaded or cross-coupled
irreps are generated support. Inside the declared enriched class, the
simultaneous zero of the class-minimal law provides the required load and
occupation. This does not prove that the unrestricted physical base--seed
parent realizes that law or exclude global or rank-novel ambient extensions.

\subsection{Pair/top exterior closure and its exact boundary}

Let $E_{\rm src}$ and $E_{\rm ax}$ be three-dimensional oriented carriers and
let $J_s:E_{\rm src}\to E_{\rm ax}$ have rank three.  The available ternary
closure is a top-form line, whereas the oriented pair defect transforms as an
axial three-dimensional representation.  Therefore
\[
 \operatorname{Hom}_{S_4}
 \bigl(V_{\rm ax},\Lambda^3V_{\rm ax}\bigr)=0,
\]
so an equivariant linear use of the top-form closure cannot produce the pair
closure.  This is a representation no-go, not a missing normalization.

The minimal common typed completion is
\[
 W_{\rm cl}^{\rm pre}
 =\Lambda^2E_{\rm ax}\oplus\Lambda^3E_{\rm ax},
 \qquad
 K_{\rm cl}=\Lambda^2J_s\oplus\Lambda^3J_s.
\]
Invertibility of $J_s$ gives ranks three and one in the two blocks.  With the
exterior metrics induced by one base metric, the once-counted action
\[
 \mathcal A_{\rm cl}^{\rm ext}
 =\frac{\mathcal A_*}{2}
 \left(
 \| (\Lambda^2J_s)^*\omega_2\|^2
 +\| (\Lambda^3J_s)^*\omega_3\|^2
 \right)
\]
has Hessian
\[
 H_{\rm cl}^{\rm ext}=\mathcal A_*K_{\rm cl}K_{\rm cl}^*\succ0.
\]
No degree-dependent gain appears.  The completion is componentwise minimal
in this graded class: deleting $\Lambda^2$ removes all three pair directions,
whereas deleting $\Lambda^3$ removes the top-form direction.

Occupation is stronger than availability.  If a pointed seed owns
$\Lambda^\bullet E_{\rm src}$ with occupied root, and a faithful Hodge base
with a source-fidelity/no-bypass law selects $J_{\rm ext}$, then
\[
 J_{\rm ext}^*J_{\rm ext}=I,
 \qquad
 \operatorname{rank}(P_2J_{\rm ext})=3,
 \qquad
 \operatorname{rank}(P_3J_{\rm ext})=1.
\]
Equivalently, for
$\mathcal D_{\rm ext}(J)=\|J^*J-I\|_{\rm HS}^2$, deletion of the degree-two
image costs three missing unit directions and deletion of degree three costs
one, while the faithful realization has zero cost.  Thus both closures are
jointly occupied in the declared connected enriched class.

The narrow relation/order seed reduct does not own the complete pointed
exterior state-and-possibility grammar, and therefore does not entail this
occupation.  A same-local-reduct component control can retain or delete one
degree.  The result closes the enriched-class realization while leaving
unrestricted physical base--seed ownership open.

\subsection*{Global-law proof obligation}

The class-minimal rank-seven packet is not by itself a theorem of global
physical occupation.  The source-fidelity/Hodge law forces every
positive-Schur additional noncontractible sector to have
\(k_{\rm eff}=0\), leaving the seed packet uniquely occupied.  However, the
common local reduct cannot prove that Nature owns this law because an
independent component zero-jet can reverse the global minimum.  Physical
promotion requires an independent source derivation or a source-frozen
discriminator, not another internal rank or Clifford closure.

\section{Conclusion}

The missing object is no longer an unspecified ``complete rank-seven
incidence.'' The declared completion has an explicit support--port preimage,
a canonical cyclic record carrier, an operator-square selector and a
nonnegative class-minimal law whose minimum realizes and occupies the packet.
The accompanying controls show separately why rank, central weights, local
marginals, action additivity and the unchanged narrow reduct do not suffice.

This is a conditional internal closure, not unrestricted parent selection.
The decisive next result must therefore be either a deeper physical derivation
of the enriched law from the actual base--seed response or a source-frozen,
gain-free cross-terminal prediction that is not implied by standard physics.
No additional standard-limit recovery can substitute for either result.
